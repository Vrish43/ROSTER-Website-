Trust & safety

Candid, not careless.

Honest information only helps when it's credible. Roster is built so athletes can speak freely and the community stays worth trusting.

  • Your identity stays separate

    Verification confirms who you are. Your posts only ever show that you're verified.

  • Moderated insights

    Every insight is reviewed against community guidelines before it reaches the community.

  • Experiences, not attacks

    Share what you lived through. Personal attacks and unverifiable claims about individuals don't get posted.
