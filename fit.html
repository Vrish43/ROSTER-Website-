Before you commit, two public signals say a lot about a program: how much it spends on its roster, and how many players walk out the door. Neither tells you if a school is right for you. Both tell you what to ask.

      Your fit checklist

      Turn the numbers into questions.

      The best fit isn't the biggest budget or the lowest turnover. It's the program where your role, your money and your development match what you were promised. Ask these before you sign.

      1. 01

        Where does the budget go?

        A big total can sit with a few stars. Ask what someone in your position and class actually received.

      2. 02

        Why did players leave?

        A coaching change, playing time and broken promises all show up as departures. Find out which one it was.

      3. 03

        Will the staff still be there?

        You're committing to people as much as a school. Ask how long your position coach plans to stay.

      4. 04

        Is it in writing?

        Verbal NIL numbers can change. Ask current players whether what they were told matched what they got.

      The data shows what happened. Athletes know why.
